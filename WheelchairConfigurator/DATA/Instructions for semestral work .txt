This week, you will focus on organizing your work for the upcoming project. There are no mandatory tasks at this stage.


Week of the 2nd Seminar – Team Formation, Project Selection, and Scheduling
* Contact your client(s) – inquire about available projects;   
* Finalize your team (if necessary); use the Discord channel to find additional members
* Register your team in the list of teams and projects (you do not need to have an assigned project yet). Indicate which project(s) your team is applying for

* Agree on meeting dates with the lecturer (by filling in the table or by e-mail in case of trouble)
